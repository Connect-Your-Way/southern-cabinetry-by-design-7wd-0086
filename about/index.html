About us

About Southern Cabinetry by Design.

Southern Cabinetry by Design is a custom cabinetmaking workshop in Robertson, turning ideas into made-to-measure kitchens and joinery for Southern Highlands homes. We focus on custom-made work — kitchens, wardrobes, laundries, vanities and storage — built to suit your space and finished to a high standard.

From the first measure-up to installation, you work directly with the people making your cabinetry, so the result fits your home and the way you use it.
